## Reference inventory

## `emmi-wu-homepage.png`

- **Source URL:** https://emmiwu.com/
- **Composition:** A large quiet field focuses attention on one off-center cluster of overlapping, rotated elements.
- **Typography:** Editorial serif copy contrasts with compact monospaced navigation and annotations.
- **Color temperature:** A warm neutral ground supports hot orange, peach, lime, and cool pale blue.
- **Texture:** Flat paper-like shapes, ruled marks, doodles, and folded corners suggest physical ephemera.
- **Density:** Most of the viewport is spacious; personality is concentrated in a small, lively area.
- **Mood:** Personal, playful, tactile, and confidently informal.
- **Do not copy literally:** The exact note-card composition, rotations, orange-and-lime palette, flower doodle, navigation labels, or biographical copy.

## `studio-dogu-browser-collage.jpeg`

- **Source URL:** https://studiodogu.com/ (no longer live, per Chloe).
- **Composition:** A wide white canvas places a central welcome message inside a loose orbit of tilted navigation labels, isolated objects, and edge-cropped fragments.
- **Typography:** A custom distorted wordmark and widely spaced geometric sans headline contrast with narrow outlined navigation labels and tiny annotations.
- **Color temperature:** Bright neutral white is interrupted by acidic chartreuse, pale beige, powder blue, lavender, and small black details.
- **Texture:** Crumpled paper forms, scanned objects, browser chrome, and low-fi cutout edges make the digital surface feel assembled by hand.
- **Density:** Sparse overall, with visual activity concentrated along the left edge and lower perimeter so the center remains breathable.
- **Mood:** Airy, surreal, playful, and exploratory—more like entering a digital studio than browsing a conventional portfolio.
- **Interaction:** The photographic objects could be dragged and repositioned. Carry forward the sense of direct manipulation and spatial play, while keeping drag behavior purposeful, keyboard-considerate, and nonessential to accessing content.
- **Do not copy literally:** The Studio Dogu name or logo, Korean copy, exact slanted menu stack, “Welcome!” lettering, individual artworks, cursor/download icon, or object placement.

## `browser-tabs-collage.jpeg`

- **Source URL:** https://x.com/3saintz/status/1982786893373698225
- **Composition:** One large landscape anchors the center while mismatched fragments cluster around its edges inside a heavy black matte, creating the feeling of many browser tabs held in one view.
- **Typography:** A direct conversational sans-serif statement establishes the premise, while oversized red script, a sticky-note aside, and tiny interface labels create competing inner voices.
- **Color temperature:** Black and white provide the frame; mossy greens, muted interiors, skin tones, and one dark red annotation keep the collage earthy rather than fluorescent.
- **Texture:** Mixed-resolution screenshots, webcam stills, annotations, archival-looking fragments, and imperfect overlaps create an intimate internet-scrapbook surface.
- **Density:** High and deliberately uneven, but the large central landscape gives the eye a stable anchor amid many small peripheral details.
- **Mood:** Associative, personal, slightly melancholic, and playfully overwhelmed—the emotional texture of an overfull browser rather than a tidy archive.
- **Do not copy literally:** The opening quote, red handwritten lettering, identifiable people, embedded artworks or film stills, clock, send button, exact black frame, or the placement and scale of any fragment.

## `fever-dream-collage.jpg`

- **Source URL:** https://x.com/3saintz/status/2061365689500213492/photo/1
- **Composition:** Two oversized, slightly misaligned advertising strips float over a full-bleed sky, with the upper strip tilted and the lower strip acting as a steadier counterweight.
- **Typography:** Condensed all-caps display lettering behaves like comic-book advertising copy, with emphatic scale changes, outlines, italics, and text that pushes against the panel edges.
- **Color temperature:** An electric cool-blue atmosphere is interrupted by saturated strawberry red, blueberry violet, hot pink, acid green, lemon yellow, and cyan.
- **Texture:** Halftone dots, photocopied fruit, airbrushed clouds, chromatic noise, hard cutout borders, and soft outer glow combine print ephemera with synthetic screen effects.
- **Density:** Each panel is optically dense and loud, but the broad cloud field gives the composition enough negative space to breathe.
- **Mood:** Dreamlike, commercial, playful, and slightly uncanny—a found advertisement drifting through a digital sky.
- **Do not copy literally:** The strawberry or blueberry imagery, prices, sales copy, exact comic-panel geometry, type treatment, cloud photograph, halftone pattern, or placement of either banner.
