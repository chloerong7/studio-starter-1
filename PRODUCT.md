# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and design studios evaluating Chloe Rong for product design roles.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Chloe as a designer focused on human interaction and meaningful impact. It should help visitors understand her people-centered perspective on design, remember her visual language and the interactivity of her site and work, and see her as a strong candidate for product design work.

Success means a visitor can quickly understand what Chloe does, what she believes about design, and why her work is relevant to a product design role. Visitors should feel encouraged to contact her.

## Positioning

Chloe places the people a product is made for at the center of her design process. Her perspective is informed by the internet, printed media, movies, and games.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The starting site map is Home, Work, one featured case study, and About. Work should prioritize product design while keeping games and graphics accessible. The featured case study is Poyjector and must cover the context and problem, process, and outcomes.

There are no special privacy or attribution requirements.

## Brand Commitments

The portfolio uses the name Chloe Rong. Its writing voice is thoughtful and playful while remaining clear for recruiters and studios. Home and About may share understated fun facts about Chloe's background and interests; work pages should keep personal details secondary to the projects.

## Evidence on Hand

Poyjector is the primary case-study evidence. Its real project material should support the problem, process, and outcomes; future work must not invent results or claims that are not present in the source material.

## Product Principles

- Put the people affected by the work at the center.
- Make Chloe's product design perspective understandable quickly.
- Make Chloe's visual language and approach to interactivity memorable.
- Show process and reasoning, not only finished outcomes.
- Balance thoughtful explanation with a playful voice.
- Preserve the truth of the work and avoid invented claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio must meet WCAG AA and work across mobile and desktop web experiences.
